## About :3
Salvage is a mod that is centered around a new item, **Metal Scrap**, which has a few different uses.

<details>
<summary>Obtaining Metal Scrap</summary>


Metal Scrap can be found around the world in structures, such as Abandoned Mineshafts, Dungeons, and in the Nether, Bastions and Fortresses.

In these structures you'll find tons of Metal Scraps, left behind from a bygone era.

![Metal Scrap in an abandoned mineshaft chest](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/87eb48fcfd3298583461db2756aab4fb39661e75.png)

You can also obtain them occasionally from zombies and obtain a good amount of them from Bartering.

</details>

<details>
<summary> What can I do with it? </summary>


Metal Scrap, being a collection of different old metal parts, have some pretty good uses.

It's main value comes from it being mostly made up of Iron. By smelting it in a furnace, you're able to repurpose it into the form of 4 Iron Ingots. With how common these are in the world, you're able to obtain plenty of iron for your needs just by exploring.

You can also craft 4 Metal Scrap into a Block of Metal Scrap, either to help you with your scrap carrying or scrap building needs. Can be crafted to stairs and slabs as well!

![Block of Metal Scrap, Metal Scrap Stairs, and Metal Scrap Slabs](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/8225af65d36f8e273fde63a571cb2c0148ffa6f4.png)

</details>

<details>
<summary> Crafting Plated Netherite </summary>


Metal Scrap also has another lucrative use: **Decorative Netherite Blocks**

By combining a Gold Ingot, Netherite Scrap, and a piece of Metal Scrap to act as a substitute, you can craft Netherite Plates.

These Netherite Plates can then be combined with Copper Blocks (which aren't oxidized) to create a Plated Block of Netherite.

Similar to Plated Jewlery, Plated Netherite is mostly made up of the Copper Block but has a layer of Netherite on top, giving it a look similar to a pure Block of Netherite. It also keeps it's block properties of being immune to explosions and being able to be pushed by pistons :3 (Be aware that it's not fire resistant tho..)

By placing 4 Plated Netherite Blocks in the crafting grid, or placing one inside of a Stonecutter, you can obtain Cut Plated Netherite, which comes with stairs and slabs!

![Plated Block of Netherite and Cut Plated Netherite](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b270fa9deedd0a39e75777095a8f55bb74c3f615.png)

Unleash the power of Goth Bathroom Tiles into the world :3

</details>


<details>
<summary> The Drill Mechanism & Handheld Drill </summary>


Sometimes inside of Bastions you'll be able to find a rare piece of forgotten technology.

The **Drill Mechanism**, is an item you can obtain either from Bastion Chests or rarely from Bartering.

You can salvage it into 4 Metal Scrap, or alternatively, craft it into the **Handheld Drill.**

The Handheld Drill used to be a powerful tool which used to excavate the depths of the Nether for it's resources. But in it's broken form, it is unable to mine on it's own.
However, the Drill is still useful. By holding it in the offhand along with a tool, the drill is able help you mine, giving you faster mining speed. With Haste 2 and the Drill, you're able to even mine tougher blocks like Deepslate instantly.

![Player holding a Handheld Drill and Netherite Pickaxe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7fa309d0c05fc9b77e54f4c657b42595ce871ace.png)

</details>


<details>
<summary> Some Other Things! </summary>


**Tetanus Death Message**: By killing an entity with a Metal Scrap, it shows the death message [Victim] forgot their tetanus shot before fighting [Attacker]

Make sure to get your Tetanus shot! (Or any important vaccination for that matter)

**New Advancements**: New advancements in the Nether Tab can be obtained by crafting a Plated Block of Netherite or a Handheld Drill :3

**Crafting Tweaks**:
- You can smelt Netherite Ingots back to Netherite Scraps in a furnace. You don't get the gold back, though.
- Crafting Cut Copper Variants from Blocks of Copper in the Crafting Table now yield 16 Cut Copper instead of 4.

</details>
